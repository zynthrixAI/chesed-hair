Questions

Before you buy

What is Chesed hair made of?

Our lace front wigs and our crochet and feather extensions are all human hair. The wigs are 100% unprocessed virgin Remy hair, so you can color, heat-style and wash them like your own.

Should I choose a wig or crochet extensions?

A lace front wig is a complete look you put on and take off in minutes. Crochet and feather extensions are installed into your own braids with a crochet needle, for a light, seamless look that stays in day and night. Not sure? Email us and we'll help you choose.

How do I choose my shade and length?

For wigs, the color guide shows all ten shades on the same model, and the length guide shows where every length from 18 to 32 inches ends on the body. For crochet, each product page shows every color on a model, in lengths from 12 to 22 inches.

How fast is shipping?

Orders ship within 1 business day by express shipping and usually arrive in 2 to 6 business days. US shipping is free on orders over $100.

What if it isn't right?

Return or exchange unused hair within 30 days, in its original packaging (wigs with the lace uncut). Your first US return label is free.
