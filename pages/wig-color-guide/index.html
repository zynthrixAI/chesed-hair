Wig Colors on Deep Skin

All ten shades on the same model, so you can compare them fairly.

  • Natural Black 1B

    Soft off-black with a faint warm brown undertone. It is the closest match to most natural hair.

    Shop black wigs

  • Jet Black 1

    The deepest, coolest black we make. It is crisp and high-contrast, with no brown at all.

    Shop black wigs

  • Virgin Natural

    Untreated hair in its natural dark brown-black, with soft espresso tones where light hits.

    Shop black wigs

  • Burgundy 530

    Rich wine red with violet depth. It glows warm on deep skin and reads dark in low light.

    Shop burgundy & 99j wigs

  • 99J Plum

    Dark plum, cooler and deeper than burgundy, almost black cherry in shadow.

    Shop burgundy & 99j wigs

  • Ginger 350

    Bright copper orange with lighter tones where the light catches it. It is our boldest warm shade.

    Shop ginger wigs

  • Honey Auburn

    Golden brown with soft honey highlights and slightly darker roots. It is warm, not orange.

    Shop auburn wigs

  • Natural Auburn

    A natural reddish brown that warms up in sunlight and stays soft indoors.

    Shop auburn wigs

  • Honey Blonde

    Warm golden blonde from root to tip, buttery rather than ash or platinum.

    Shop honey blonde wigs

  • Red

    True cherry red from root to tip, with no orange and no pink.

    Shop red wigs

Shades people mix up

1B vs Jet Black 1 vs Virgin Natural. 1B is soft off-black with a warm undertone, the closest match to most natural hair. Jet Black 1 is the deepest, coolest black. Virgin Natural is untreated dark brown-black and the best base if you plan to dye.

Burgundy 530 vs 99J Plum. 530 is a brighter wine red. 99J is darker and cooler, almost black cherry in shadow.

Honey Blonde vs Honey Auburn. Honey Blonde is golden blonde from root to tip. Honey Auburn is golden brown with honey highlights.
