About Chesed Hair

Chesed means lovingkindness.

Chesed is the Hebrew word for a deep, unconditional love. We named the brand after it because that's how hair should be made: with care for the woman who wears it and the people who make it.

Every Chesed unit starts as 100% unprocessed virgin Remy human hair, sourced and made without exploited labor.
