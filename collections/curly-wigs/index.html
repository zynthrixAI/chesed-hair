About curly wigs

Our curly lace front wigs have small, dense spiral curls that look full from root to tip. They are made from 100% unprocessed virgin Remy human hair and come in 9 shades and lengths from 10 to 32 inches. Curly units give the most volume of any Chesed texture, so a shorter length still reads big. Refresh the curls with water and a leave-in conditioner between washes.

Questions

Curly Wigs: questions, answered

How do I keep the curls defined?

Wet the hair, apply a leave-in conditioner and let it air-dry. Avoid brushing curls when dry.

Does a curly wig look shorter?

Yes. Curls shrink, so a curly wig sits 2 to 4 inches higher than its stretched length.

Can I wear it straight?

You can blow it out and flat iron it. The curls return when the hair gets wet.

How full is a curly wig?

Curly units look the fullest of all our textures because each curl takes up more space.
