    350 Wigs: questions, answered

    Is 350 orange or red?

    Orange-copper. It is brighter and more orange than auburn.

    How do I keep 350 bright?

    Use color-safe shampoo and cool water.

    Which textures come in 350?

    Body wave, curly, kinky straight, loose deep wave and straight.

    Does 350 suit dark skin?

    Yes. It is one of the most popular bold shades on deep skin.
