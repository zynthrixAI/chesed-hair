    Questions

    Questions, answered

    What are Chesed wigs made of?

    Every Chesed wig is 100% unprocessed virgin Remy human hair on a lace front, so you can color, heat-style and wash it like your own hair.

    What lace and cap do the wigs have?

    A 13x5 lace front for a natural hairline, on a cap that is one size fits all.

    How do I choose my length?

    Use the length guide: it shows where each length from 18 to 32 inches ends on the body. Waves and curls sit about 2 inches higher than straight hair.

    How do I choose my shade?

    Every shade is shown on the same model in real rooms. The color guide puts all ten side by side, including the ones people mix up, like 1B vs 1.

    What if the wig isn't right?

    Return or exchange unused wigs within 30 days with the lace uncut. Your first US return label is free.
