    Questions

    Natural Black (1B) Wigs: questions, answered

    Which textures come in Natural Black (1B)?

    Body wave, curly, kinky straight, loose deep wave, straight, virgin curl, permed straight, wet & wavy and deep wave.

    What is the difference between 1B and 1?

    1B is off-black with a warm brown undertone. 1 is jet black, cooler and darker.

    Can I dye Natural Black (1B)?

    Virgin is the best base for color because it has never been processed. We recommend a professional colorist for any color change.

    How do I keep Natural Black (1B) looking its best?

    Wash in cool water with sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner, and store the wig on a stand.
