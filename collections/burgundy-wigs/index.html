Questions

Burgundy & 99J Wigs: questions, answered

What is the difference between 530 and 99J?

530 is a brighter wine red. 99J is a darker, cooler plum.

Does burgundy fade?

Wash in cool water with a color-safe shampoo to keep the shade rich.

Which textures come in burgundy?

Burgundy 530 comes in six textures. 99J Plum is in body wave.

Is burgundy good for dark skin?

Yes. It is one of the most flattering bold shades on deep skin.
