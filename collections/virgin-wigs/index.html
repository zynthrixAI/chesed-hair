    Questions

    Virgin Wigs: questions, answered

    Which textures come in Virgin?

    Body wave, kinky straight, loose deep wave, straight, virgin curl, permed straight, wet & wavy and deep wave.

    How is Virgin different from 1B?

    Virgin is untreated hair in its natural dark brown-black, softer than 1 and a touch warmer than 1B.

    Can I dye Virgin?

    Virgin is the best base for color because it has never been processed. We recommend a professional colorist for any color change.

    How do I keep Virgin looking its best?

    Wash in cool water with sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner, and store the wig on a stand.
