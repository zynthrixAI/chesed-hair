About virgin curl wigs

Virgin curl wigs are curly units left in their natural, uncolored state, so the hair has never been processed. That makes them the best base if you plan to dye the wig yourself later.

They come in 1B, 1 and Virgin, in lengths from 10 to 32 inches, as lace front units in 100% unprocessed virgin Remy human hair.

    Questions

    Virgin Curl Wigs: questions, answered

    What makes virgin curl different from curly?

    Virgin curl is never colored, so it stays in its natural shade. That makes it the safest base for dyeing.

    Can I dye it?

    Yes. Unprocessed hair takes color best. We recommend a professional colorist.

    Which shades are available?

    1B, 1 and Virgin.

    How do I care for it?

    Treat it like natural curly hair: moisture, gentle detangling, air-drying.
