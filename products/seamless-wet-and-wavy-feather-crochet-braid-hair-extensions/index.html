Questions

Questions about crochet & feather extensions

What are feather crochet extensions?

They are hair extensions made to be installed with a crochet technique, so the finished look is seamless and light.

How many packs do I need for a full head?

It depends on the style and the length you choose. Some listings offer a 3-pack full-head option. Ask us if you are not sure.

Can I dye or restyle them?

They can be, but it is not recommended, because the hair is already styled and colored.

How long do they last?

With care they can last up to a year. Textured styles need regular upkeep.

How do I choose a color and length?

Each style comes in six colors, from natural black to honey blonde and ruby wine, and in lengths from 12 to 22 inches. Pick a color on the product page to see it on a model.

How fast will it ship, and can I return it?

Orders ship within 1 business day and usually arrive in 2 to 6 business days. Unused extensions in their original packaging can be returned or exchanged within 30 days.
