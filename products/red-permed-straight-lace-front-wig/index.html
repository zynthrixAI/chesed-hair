About this shade

True cherry red from root to tip, with no orange and no pink. Shown here on the same model as every other Chesed wig, so you can compare shades fairly.

Details

Texture: Permed Straight. Hair: 100% unprocessed virgin Remy human hair. Lace: 13x5 lace front. Cap: one size fits all. Lengths: 18 to 36 inches.

How to install

Braid or flatten your hair and put on a wig cap. Place the wig, line up the lace with your hairline, trim the lace, then secure with glue, tape or the adjustable band. Finish by laying the baby hairs.

Care

Wash every 7 to 10 wears in cool water with sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner. Use a heat protectant before styling. Store on a stand.

The texture · Permed Straight

Straight with a soft perm texture and extra body.

Permed straight falls straight but has a soft perm texture through it, so it looks fuller and more natural than glassy, silky straight hair. It is a good match for relaxed or lightly textured natural hair and keeps its body between washes. Each Chesed permed straight wig is a lace front unit in 100% unprocessed virgin Remy human hair, in 8 shades and lengths from 18 to 36 inches.

Questions

Questions about permed straight

How is permed straight different from straight?

Straight is smooth and silky. Permed straight has a soft texture and more body, so it looks less flat.

Can I curl a permed straight wig?

Yes. Use a heat protectant and a curling iron or wand.

Does it blend with my natural hair?

It blends best with relaxed, silk-pressed or lightly textured hair.
