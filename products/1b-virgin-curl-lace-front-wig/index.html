About this shade

Soft off-black with a faint warm brown undertone. It is the closest match to most natural hair. Shown here on the same model as every other Chesed wig, so you can compare shades fairly.

Details

Texture: Virgin Curl. Hair: 100% unprocessed virgin Remy human hair. Lace: 13x5 lace front. Cap: one size fits all. Lengths: 18 to 36 inches.

How to install

Braid or flatten your hair and put on a wig cap. Place the wig, line up the lace with your hairline, trim the lace, then secure with glue, tape or the adjustable band. Finish by laying the baby hairs.

Care

Wash every 7 to 10 wears in cool water with sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner. Air-dry to keep the pattern defined. Store on a stand.

The texture · Virgin Curl

Natural-color curls in untreated virgin hair.

Virgin curl wigs are curly units left in their natural, uncolored state, so the hair has never been processed. That makes them the best base if you plan to dye the wig yourself later. They come in 1B, 1 and Virgin, in lengths from 18 to 36 inches, as lace front units in 100% unprocessed virgin Remy human hair.

Questions

Questions about virgin curl

What makes virgin curl different from curly?

Virgin curl is never colored, so it stays in its natural shade. That makes it the safest base for dyeing.

Can I dye it?

Yes. Unprocessed hair takes color best. We recommend a professional colorist.

Which shades are available?
