The texture · Loose Deep Wave

Loose, defined curls between a wave and a curl.

Loose deep wave sits between a body wave and a curl: defined, bouncy curls that are looser than a deep wave and fuller than a body wave. It is a strong choice if you want volume without tight curls. Every Chesed loose deep wave wig is a lace front unit made from 100% unprocessed virgin Remy human hair, in 9 shades and lengths from 18 to 32 inches.

Questions

Questions about loose deep wave

Is loose deep wave the same as body wave?

No. Loose deep wave has more defined, curlier waves. Body wave is softer and looser.

How do I keep the pattern?

Wash, apply a curl cream and let it air-dry or diffuse.

Does it tangle?

Less than tighter curls. Detangle wet with a wide-tooth comb.
