About this shade

Untreated hair in its natural dark brown-black, with soft espresso tones where light hits. Shown here on the same model as every other Chesed wig, so you can compare shades fairly.

Details

Texture: Curly. Hair: 100% unprocessed virgin Remy human hair. Lace: 13x5 lace front. Cap: one size fits all. Lengths: 18 to 32 inches.

How to install

Braid or flatten your hair and put on a wig cap. Place the wig, line up the lace with your hairline, trim the lace, then secure with glue, tape or the adjustable band. Finish by laying the baby hairs.

Care

Wash every 7 to 10 wears in cool water with sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner. Air-dry to keep the pattern defined. Store on a stand.

The texture · Curly

Small, dense spiral curls with full volume.

Our curly lace front wigs have small, dense spiral curls that look full from root to tip. They are made from 100% unprocessed virgin Remy human hair and come in 9 shades and lengths from 18 to 32 inches. Curly units give the most volume of any Chesed texture, so a shorter length still reads big. Refresh the curls with water and a leave-in conditioner between washes.

Questions

Questions about curly

How do I keep the curls defined?

Wet the hair, apply a leave-in conditioner and let it air-dry. Avoid brushing curls when dry.

Does a curly wig look shorter?

Yes. Curls shrink, so a curly wig sits 2 to 4 inches higher than its stretched length.

Can I wear it straight?

You can blow it out and flat iron it. The curls return when the hair gets wet.
