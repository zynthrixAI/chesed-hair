About this shade

Warm golden blonde from root to tip, buttery rather than ash or platinum. Shown here on the same model as every other Chesed wig, so you can compare shades fairly.

Details

Texture: Straight. Hair: 100% unprocessed virgin Remy human hair. Lace: lace front (lace size to confirm). Density: to confirm. Cap: to confirm. Lengths: 10 to 32 inches.

How to install

Braid or flatten your hair and put on a wig cap. Place the wig, line up the lace with your hairline, trim the lace, then secure with glue, tape or the adjustable band. Finish by laying the baby hairs.

Care

Wash every 7 to 10 wears in cool water with sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner. Use a heat protectant before styling. Store on a stand.

About straight

Our straight lace front wigs are smooth, silky and swing with natural movement. Straight hair shows length most clearly, so it is the best texture for long lengths up to 32 inches.
