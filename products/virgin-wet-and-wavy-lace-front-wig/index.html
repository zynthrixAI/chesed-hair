How to install

Braid or flatten your hair and put on a wig cap. Place the wig, line up the lace with your hairline, trim the lace, then secure with glue, tape or the adjustable band. Finish by laying the baby hairs.

Care

Wash every 7 to 10 wears in cool water with sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner. Air-dry to keep the pattern defined. Store on a stand.

Questions

Questions about wet & wavy

How do I keep the waves defined?

Wet the hair, add a leave-in conditioner and let it air-dry or diffuse. Avoid brushing when dry.

Is wet & wavy the same as body wave?

No. Wet & wavy has a looser, glossier, more water-like pattern. Body wave is a softer S-shape.

Does it look shorter than its length?

Yes. The waves take up length, so it sits about 2 inches higher than straight hair.
