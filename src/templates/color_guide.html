1B vs 1 vs Virgin

1B is soft off-black with a warm undertone, the closest match to most natural hair. 1 is the deepest, coolest black. Virgin is untreated dark brown-black and the best base if you plan to dye.

530 vs 99J

530 is a brighter wine red. 99J is darker and cooler, almost black cherry in shadow.

Honey Blonde vs Honey Auburn

Honey Blonde is golden blonde from root to tip. Honey Auburn is golden brown with honey highlights.
