How wig length is measured

Wig length is measured from the crown to the ends with the hair pulled straight. Straight hair falls at its full length. Waves and curls spring up, so a wavy or curly wig sits about 2 inches higher on the body, and tight coils like kinky curly can sit 4 inches higher or more.

Which length should I choose?

For an everyday look, most people choose 16 to 20 inches. For drama and volume, go 24 inches or longer. Straight textures show length most clearly. Curly textures look fuller, so a shorter curly wig still reads big.
