How wig length is measured

Wig length is measured from the crown to the ends with the hair pulled straight. Straight hair falls at its full length. Waves and curls spring up, so a wavy or curly wig sits about 2 inches higher on the body, and tight coils like kinky curly can sit 4 inches higher or more. The photos are based on an average height of about 5'5". If you're taller, each length lands a little higher. If you're shorter, a little lower.

Which length should I choose?

For an everyday look, most people choose 18 to 22 inches. For drama and volume, go 26 inches or longer. Straight textures show length most clearly. Curly textures look fuller, so a shorter curly wig still reads big.
